FAQ

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.

I am stuck on a puzzle. Tap Hint, or Undo a few moves — the coach tells you when a route can no longer get everyone home.

Does the game need the internet? No. Monster Lift plays fully offline and has no ads or in-app purchases.
